# My Weekly Planner 📓

A cozy, notebook-style weekly planner with journaling, habits, stickers, and cloud sync.

## Getting Started

### Open the App

**Live app:** [https://your-deployed-url.com](https://journal-planner.vercel.app)

**Add to Home Screen (mobile):**
1. Open the app in your phone's browser (Chrome on Android, Safari on iPhone)
2. Tap the share/menu button
3. Select **"Add to Home Screen"**
4. The app will open like a native app with no browser bars

## Features

### Left Page — Your Week at a Glance

- **Day rows** — Each day (Mon–Sun) shows a checklist of tasks
- **Check items** — Tap the checkbox to mark tasks done
- **Add items** — Tap **"+ add item"** at the bottom of any day
- **Add notes** — Tap **"add note..."** below the checklist
- **Select a day** — Tap any day row to view its journal on the right page
- **Friday** — Split into two columns: plain notes (left) and checkboxes (right)
- **Saturday** — Shows a star sticker and an editable special note
- **Sunday** — Rest day (empty)

### Right Page — Journal & Habits

- **Journal** — Free-text area to write about your day
- **Mood line** — Log your mood or calorie note
- **Music note** — Track what you listened to and which playlist
- **Todo card** — Separate to-do list with its own title and checklist
- **Habit areas** — Track daily habits (dance, gym, stretch, etc.)
  - Tap a cell to cycle: empty → ✓ → ✗ → empty
  - Tap **"+ add habit"** to create new ones
- **Water tracker** — Tap droplets to log water intake (each = 0.45L)

### Stickers ⭐

1. Tap the **⭐ sticker button** (bottom-right corner)
2. **Choose a page** — Tap "📋 left" or "📓 right" to pick which page
3. **Place a sticker** — Tap any sticker in the tray to place it
4. **Move** — Drag a placed sticker to reposition it
5. **Rotate** — Select a sticker, then drag the blue rotate handle
6. **Resize** — Select a sticker, then drag the green resize handle
7. **Delete** — Select a sticker, then tap the pink × button
8. **Custom stickers** — Upload your own images via the "+ upload sticker" button
9. **Drag and drop** — On desktop, drag stickers from the tray directly onto the page

### Week Navigation

- **Left/right arrows** in the header to go to the previous or next week
- The current week number and date range are shown in the center

### Theme

- Tap the **sun/moon button** (top-right of left page) to toggle dark/light mode
- Your preference is remembered between sessions

## Data & Sync

- Everything saves **automatically** to the cloud as you type
- A small indicator in the top-left shows save status (saving... / saved ✓)
- Your data is **private** — only you can see your journal entries
- Sign out with the **"sign out"** button (top-right on desktop, bottom-left on mobile)

## Tips

- **Mobile** — Use the bottom nav (📋 Days / 📓 Journal) to switch between views
- **Stickers persist** — Your placed stickers stay in the same spot every time you visit
- **Habit names are editable** — Tap any habit name to rename it
- **Todo card title is editable** — Tap the title to change it (e.g. "monday todo")

## Browser Support

Works best on modern browsers:
- Chrome / Edge (desktop & mobile)
- Safari (iPhone)
- Firefox

For the best experience, add it to your home screen as a PWA (see "Getting Started" above).
